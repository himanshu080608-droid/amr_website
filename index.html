DDSM115 Direct-Drive Smart Hub Motors

Traditional mobile robots rely on planetary or spur gearboxes that develop backlash over time, degrading odometry calculations during reversals. Ros_lidar_bot integrates DDSM115 direct-drive smart motors where the high-traction rubber wheel is mounted directly onto the motor's outer rotor.

✔
Integrated Driver & Encoder: Internal 3-phase FOC driver, magnetic rotary encoder, and thermal protection built directly inside each hub.
✔
RS485 Half-Duplex Bus: Addressed via IDs (0x01 for Left, 0x02 for Right) with strict 2 ms inter-query guard timing to prevent bus arbitration clash.
✔
Low-Speed Threshold: Smooth linear control down to ≈6 RPM. Below 6 RPM, the motor deadband requires customized velocity feedforward ramps.

Bosch BNO055 9-DOF IMU + Dedicated Mega

Achieving stable SLAM in indoor environments requires sub-millisecond orientation tracking. The Bosch BNO055 fuses a triaxial 14-bit accelerometer, 16-bit gyroscope, and geomagnetic sensor through its onboard BSX sensor fusion coprocessor.

✔
Arduino Mega Serial Offloader: The Mega reads orientation quaternions and angular velocity over hardware I2C/UART and streams to the Pi at 500,000 baud.
✔
Timestamp Determinism: Offloading sensor polling to a dedicated MCU guarantees jitter-free 50 Hz ROS 2 sensor_msgs/Imu messages.
✔
EKF Fusion: Fuses with /odom_raw in ekf.yaml to feed Nav2 and SLAM Toolbox with audited odom → base_footprint transforms.

Strict Voltage Isolation Warning

Never power the 20kg OT5320M pan servo from the Arduino Uno 5V pin! Stall currents will brown out the microcontroller and corrupt the serial link. Always tie Servo GND, Battery GND, and Uno GND together.

COMMISSIONING MANUAL

Hardware Bring-Up & Calibration Steps

Official sequential procedure to calibrate sensors and launch the physical robot.

01

Static IP Network Configuration for RPLidar S2E

The S2E communicates over Ethernet UDP. Assign a static IP on the Pi's eth0 port matching the LiDAR's subnet:

sudo nmcli con add type ethernet ifname eth0 con-name lidar ipv4.method manual ipv4.addresses 192.168.11.1/24
ping 192.168.11.2
02

Flash Microcontroller Firmware

Flash the Arduino Uno and Mega sketches before launching ROS 2:

  • Arduino Uno: Flash arduino/camera_head/camera_head.ino to establish 115,200 baud ASCII servo control.
  • Arduino Mega: Flash BNO055 high-speed serial firmware streaming at 500,000 baud.
03

Bench Motor Verification (Without Safety Filter)

Test wheel rotation on a stand before autonomous movement:

ros2 run Ros_lidar_bot driver_node --ros-args -p cmd_vel_topic:=/cmd_vel
04

Full Hardware Bringup

Start the hardware drivers, safety stop, SLAM Toolbox, and web dashboard:

ros2 launch Ros_lidar_bot launch_robot.launch.py

Then connect to http://<robot-ip>:8080 in your browser.

FIELD REPAIR GUIDE

Hardware Diagnostics & Troubleshooting

Direct solutions to known hardware failure states and bus issues.

Root Cause: A stale previous launch instance is locking the serial ports (/dev/ttyACM*) or HTTP port 8080.

Fix: Kill all lingering nodes and verify port release:

pkill -9 -f "ros2 launch"; pkill -9 -f _node
fuser -v /dev/ttyACM* 8080/tcp

Root Cause: driver_node.py strictly listens to /cmd_vel_safe, not /cmd_vel. If safety_stop_node has not started or an obstacle is within min_safe_distance (0.40m), all forward/reverse commands are zeroed.

Check: Verify command flow with ros2 topic hz /cmd_vel_safe and check /safety_blocked.

Root Cause: LiDAR laser rays are bouncing off the robot chassis, tripping the safety stop node.

Fix: Ensure ignore_below in safety_stop_node.py is set to at least the chassis radius (0.28 m).

Root Cause: Due to dense point-cloud volume (~3200 pts/rev), scan-matching runs ~0.6s behind on the Pi 5 during rapid rotations.

Fix: Maintain transform_tolerance: 1.0 in nav2_params.yaml and keep angular yaw velocity under 0.8 rad/s during initial mapping.
